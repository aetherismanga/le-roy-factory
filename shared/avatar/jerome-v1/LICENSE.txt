Jérôme — personalized RANDAL visit avatar
Geometry and rig adapted from Microsoft Rocketbox · Male_Adult_01.
Source: https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox
80-bone rig and four existing facial morph targets preserved.
Head/body albedo textures are new image-generated UV edits based on the authorized
appearance reference. Geometry UVs and trouser/scalp/chin shape are modified.
Original normal maps resized to 2048 pixels and converted to WebP.
This is an interpreted avatar, not a photographic facial scan.
